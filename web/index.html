How it works

A neural network called MobileNetV2 was already trained to recognise shapes and textures. Its last layer was retrained on 1,768 photos from the TrashNet dataset to tell six types of waste apart.

The model runs inside your browser, so your photo isn’t uploaded anywhere.

What it can’t do

  • It learned from single items on plain white backgrounds, so real photos may score lower.
  • It always picks one of the six types, even for things that aren’t waste.
  • The bin names and colours are examples, not an official standard.

The experiments behind it
